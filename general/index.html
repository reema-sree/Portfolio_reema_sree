GOOD TO KNOW

A few common questions.

Have a different question? Send me a note and we’ll figure it out.

How long does a project take?＋

It depends on the scope. A landing page often takes 1–2 weeks; an MVP can take 3–6 weeks. I’ll confirm a realistic timeline after we define the work.

How do revisions work?＋

We’ll agree on review points and revision rounds in the project scope. I share working versions early so feedback arrives while changes are still easy to make.

How is payment handled?＋

Payment milestones and due dates are agreed in writing before work starts. The exact schedule depends on the project scope.

What timezone do you work in?＋

I’m based in Hyderabad, India (IST). I work remotely and can agree on meeting times that overlap with your team.
